The two files are OpenAI's ChatGPT logo, as OpenAI publishes it for "Continue with ChatGPT" buttons. They were downloaded unchanged on 2026-11-08 from:

- `https://developers.openai.com/assets/siwc/sign-in-buttons/chatgpt-logo-black.svg` (SHA-256 `b8b9aba7ba591f54bf1805f422bc23f32c44ee993643cc378cc77f766061f7a1`)

The logo is OpenAI's trademark. Mannie's Apache-2.0 license does not cover these files. Mannie uses them only in the sign-in button, in one of the formats that OpenAI's guidelines approve (<https://developers.openai.com/siwc/ui-ux-guidelines>, verified 2026-11-08): the black logo on light backgrounds and the white logo on dark ones, beside the words "Continue with ChatGPT". Replace them only with newer files from the same source.
