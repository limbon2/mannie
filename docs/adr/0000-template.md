## Context

The forces at play: requirements, constraints, risks and earlier decisions. State facts, with versions, sources and the date they were verified when they matter.

## Decision

What we will do, stated plainly and specifically enough to act on: versions, configuration, boundaries, and what is excluded.

## Consequences

- Positive: what gets easier or safer.
- Negative: what gets harder, what we give up and what it costs.
- Risks: what could make this decision wrong, how we would notice, and what limits the damage.
